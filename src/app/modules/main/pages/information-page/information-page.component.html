Information.

SecurePay has extensive reporting and auditing capabilties so you can find out exactly what's been sent to each customer, who's made a payment and who's having difficulty paying.

Reports include:

  • Payment requests created for a given time period
  • Emails that have been sent out to customers
  • Payment transactions made — both successful and failures
  • Workflow queue monitoring

Access to reports is restricted based on the authorities you give to each of your SecurePay users.
