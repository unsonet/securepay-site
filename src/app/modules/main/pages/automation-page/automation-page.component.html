Automation.

SecurePay's workflow engine allows you to automatically send payment requests and follow-up reminders at set intervals — with optional payment deadlines and recurring transactions.

You're automatically advised whenever payments are made, when a payer is having problems paying or if a payer hasn't responded to the payment request by a predetermined deadline.

SecurePay can even extract data from any other IT or accounting systems you may have to generate payment requests automatically.

All payment requests sent, outstanding workflow actions and payments received are visible from the Workflow Dashboard.
